The mart

Only horological items are allowed at the mart. No antique knives, coins, jewelry or other items not associated with horology… especially fake watches.

Non-members pay a $10 admission, which includes the option to buy and sell at the mart.

What to bring

Please bring your Chapter 5 badge and NAWCC membership card. Expired memberships will need to be renewed before entry.
