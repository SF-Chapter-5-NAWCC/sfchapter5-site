Join or renew

Chapter dues are $25 ($26 with spouse), including one badge. Chapter members must also be members of the national NAWCC.

Guests are welcome: admission for non-members is $10 and includes buying and selling at the mart.

How to join

Our namesake

Dr. W. Barclay Stephens

The chapter is named in memory of Dr. W. Barclay Stephens, the great horologist and philanthropist. Price Russ has compiled an extensive collection of writing and information about him: his life, his horological collection and his scrapbook.
